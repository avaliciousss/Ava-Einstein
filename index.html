Your Questions, Answered

The Furlan Group brings 26+ years of combined experience to Sarasota's luxury real estate market. Led by Brandi Furlan, our team has completed 462+ transactions with $20M+ in annual volume. Ken Anderson joined in 2024, bringing data-driven precision and fresh energy to our established track record of success.

We employ a comprehensive data analysis approach, examining comparable sales, current market trends, days on market statistics, and architectural features. Our pricing strategy balances market data with property-specific attributes to position your home competitively while maximizing value.

As part of Keller Williams powered by PLACE, we leverage professional photography, drone videography, virtual staging, 3D tours, targeted digital advertising across social media and major real estate platforms. Our CRM system ensures immediate response to inquiries, while our analytics track engagement to refine strategy in real-time.

Our team structure provides multiple points of expertise while maintaining Ken as your primary contact. You benefit from Brandi's 26 years of market knowledge, our collective network of buyers and sellers, and our coordinated approach to marketing and negotiation. It's personalized service backed by team resources.

We serve Sarasota, Venice, Nokomis, Bradenton, Longboat Key, Lakewood Ranch, and surrounding communities across Sarasota and Manatee counties. Our expertise spans luxury waterfront estates, single-family homes, condos, new construction, and investment properties.
